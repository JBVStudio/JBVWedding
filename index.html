Visual Expectations

Design language & visual requests, moment by moment

Design Language

One consistent, off-white / neutral floral palette runs through the entire day — welcome, ceremony, cocktail hour, and dinner all draw from the same tone of flowers. Nothing dark or mismatched; every arrangement should read as part of the same family.

Welcome Reception

  • Mirror with the custom welcome messaging/design
  • Off-white/neutral flower placement, consistent with the rest of the wedding
  • Basket for the umbrellas — confirm whether we need to bring one
  • Janan & Juan to bring the sun umbrellas for the basket

Ceremony

  • No chairs for the couple — Juan, Janan & officiant Nabeel stand; will need a mic
  • Flower style as circled in the reference photo — off-white/neutral, consistent with the rest
  • Flowers by all chairs in the aisle, not just three — similar to the reference photo. These will be moved and reused for the party area by the LED sign (see Reception / Party section).
  • Rose petals in baskets for the four flower girls
  • Bridal bouquet as shown in the reference photo

  • Off-white, neutral flowers on tables — consistent with the rest of the flowers
  • White fabric napkins (Janan & Juan to bring) for the entrance twirl moment
NotesFlamenco band placement — needs to be visible, up with guests on the second level, not at the bottom.

Dinner

  • Mirror styled with flowers & candles at the base; guest seating arrangement displayed on the mirror — the seating arrangement needs to be printed
